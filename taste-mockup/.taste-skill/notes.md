## 3. Direction
Dealt (seed 1191904537): one-motif / borrowed-category / page-is-the-object. Chose page-is-the-object: a Chinese church already prints its Sunday in two scripts (programme, monthly newsletter, Connect card, offering envelope); all of MPCBC's real content is that kind of information (times, rooms, names, addresses), so the object holds it without invention, and flat panels + aligned columns rebuild well in Elementor. one-motif would hang on a lancet/gable shape every chapel has; borrowed-category fights the audience (families, elders).
Redraw: hero (Striped card beside the room → Card over a dim room) because the brief requires a full-screen looping background video; the split hero holds only half-width media. Nav bar kept (blueprint says none) because a church home needs its sections in navigation (house rule).
Palette kept (Newsprint and brass): newsprint grey/near-black is the bulletin itself; not the building's cream/brown (that would be the banned cream+terracotta).

## 4. Idea
On this page, **MPCBC's Sunday morning — Mandarin at 9, children at 10, Cantonese and English at 11, under one steeple at 302 W. Commonwealth — becomes its printed programme**: a bilingual Sunday card over the film, a timetable you set to your language, a reply card, and an envelope addressed to the church.
Competitor test: another SGV Chinese church prints a bulletin, but not these hours, rooms, names, this steeple or this address.
Live thing: "Your Sunday" picker — choose 粵語 / English / 國語 and the timetable picks your service, room, pastor, archive link and counts down to the next one (Pacific time); it also preselects your service on the reply card. No-script fallback: all four times are listed.

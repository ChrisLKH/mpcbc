Our story

教會歷史

Founded in 1977, MPCBC began as a Cantonese-speaking congregation.

During the 1980s, as the Mandarin-speaking immigrant community in Los Angeles grew rapidly, the church recognized the need to share the gospel and provide a spiritual home for these new immigrants. In response, MPCBC established its Mandarin Ministry, holding its first Mandarin worship service in 1988.

In 1991, MPCBC expanded its ministry by introducing an English worship service. Since then, the church has continued to worship in three languages, serving different language communities while remaining united as one church in Christ.

What we believe

我們的信仰

We believe in Jesus, God’s only Son, who died on a cross for our sins, was raised to life, and will one day return. We believe in the Trinity: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We teach the Bible, believing that it is from God, entirely true, and relevant to our present age.

How to Wise Up
June 21, 2020June 20, 2020 by english
During days such as these, it is important to find one’s “foundation.” Question, What is it in your life life that really GROUNDS you as a person? I believe we need grounding not just in slogans, opinions nor certainly any political rhetoric. We REALLY need rock-solid convictions from above found in GOD’S wisdom. Wisdom is what the Book of Proverbs is all about. We are beginning a new 8 week series on this precious book of the Bible. God’s practical wisdom beings all kinds of benefits to life.
Join us for worship on Sunday, 6/21/2020 at 11:00 PM Pacific Time  as I preach the sermon, “HOW TO WISE UP.”

January 2026 Newsletter
Welcome, friend! Whether you’ve been walking with Connect Ministry for years or you’re just getting to know us, this newsletter is one way we stay connected as a church family. Inside, you’ll find encouraging stories, a heartfelt devotional, updates from our missionaries in Paris, and reflections on what God is doing in and through our community here in Alhambra.
We hope this brings you a sense of encouragement—and maybe even a reminder that you belong here, just as you are.

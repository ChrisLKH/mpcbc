Fellowship, Bible Study, and Prayer
Walking with God can be challenging, but you don’t have to do it alone. Studying together, praying together, and encouraging one another is a great way to strengthen your faith and keep your life deeply rooted in Christ. That is why Connect Ministry offers a variety of fellowship groups for you to participate in. All are welcome.
Please join us for your daily dose of Christian encouragement.
Adult Bible Study and Prayer Meeting
Every Thursday, 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM
Experience spiritual growth and fellowship with Deacon Charles Leung at our virtual Adult Bible study and prayer meeting. Join us to deepen your understanding of Bible-based doctrine and learn how to apply it to your life.
We meet every Wednesday at 7:00 PM (PST) on Zoom.
College/Career Fellowship
Every Monday, 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM
Join us at MPCBC’s College & Career Fellowship led by Deacon Jeff Cha & Brother Anson Hsu. Connect with like-minded young adults in a relaxed, supportive setting. Navigate life’s challenges together and cultivate a strong Christian foundation.
This fellowship meets in West Covina.
Youth Sunday School
Every Sunday, 10:00 AM – 10:45 AM
Join Deacon Evelyn Hsu at MPCBC for Connect Ministry’s Youth Sunday School fellowship! New believers & curious teens learn how to live a Christian life. This is a great opportunity for your teens to learn more about the Bible and how it applies to their lives.
This group meets in the education annex at 302 W. Commonwealth Ave.
English Sunday School
Every Sunday, 10:00 AM – 10:45 AM
All are welcome!
Join this diverse group for a little studying, some interesting discussions, and a lot of supportive fellowship.
This group meets in the Fellowship Hall.

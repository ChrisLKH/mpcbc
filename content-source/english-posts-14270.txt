July 2026 Newsletter
Summer is here, and so is another chance to gather around stories of God’s goodness. This month’s newsletter is filled with testimonies that remind us how faithful He has been—through seasons of uncertainty, miraculous healing, and even unexpected turns on the mission field.
Whether you’ve been part of our Connect family for decades or you’re just discovering us, we hope these pages encourage your heart. God is at work. And you’re part of the story.
Browse the newsletter
Take a few moments to flip through this month’s issue. You’ll find personal testimonies, reflections on God’s provision, and glimpses of what He’s doing through missions—near and far.

What's Inside This Issue
A Personal Testimony of Faith – One member shares how the message of John 9 changed everything—and how God’s grace carried her through seasons of struggle and job loss
Miracles in the Midst of Hardship – Stories of God’s provision through unemployment, a serious car accident, and unexpected healing
Missions Adventures in Mongolia and Hong Kong – From car accidents to last-minute plan changes, see how God showed up in amazing ways on the mission field
Trusting God Through the Unknowns – Reflections on tithing, faith, and the faithfulness of a God who never stops providing
A Glimpse of God's Faithfulness
"We were never without provisions. We had a home, we had food, and we were never without money. We kept two homes afloat, purchased plane tickets for visiting—but we made it a point to constantly tithe, for God would provide. And God continued to provide."
This is just one of the powerful stories you’ll find inside. Real people. Real faith. A very real God.

Mission Statement: 
Knowing God is our foundation, glorifying God is our goal. Through 
worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and evangelism, we seek 
to know God, and make His name known. 
 
 
Core Values: 
 
Christ as the Head of the Church – Ephesians 1:22-23; 2:19-21 
 
We value Christ as the head of our lives and as the foundation of our 
church.  We submit ourselves to his direction, guidance and control. 
 
Biblical Teaching – 2 Timothy 3:16-17 and 2 Timothy 4:2 
 
We value Biblical teaching because the Holy Bible is the complete, 
inerrant and infallible word of God to all mankind.  It is the foundation of 
our beliefs and it is proclaimed from our pulpit, taught in our classes and 
honored by our obedience. 
 
Worship – Romans 12:1 
 
We value worship that draws us closer to God’s presence.  Whether 
whispering our personal desires for him or passionately singing his praise, 
we declare God is worthy. 
 
Prayer – Matthew 6:9-13, Philippians 4:6, 19 
 
We value prayer as our means of communicating intimately and 
reverently with God. 
 
Evangelism – Acts 1:8, Matthew 28:19-20 
 
We value Evangelism because we love people and want to see them come 
to know Christ as their Lord and Savior. 
 
Community –Acts 2:44-47 
 
We value a sense of community in order to develop relationships that 
nurture, support and teach others to be Christ-like. 
 
Discipleship – 2 Timothy 2:1-2; John 8:31 
 
We value discipleship that leads to authentic life change.  It is our goal to 
develop followers of Christ who grow and live in the knowledge of Him 
and His teachings.

TESTIMONY OF YESENIA CARILLO 
 
As a child, I was raised as a catholic. But, it’s never 
been a source of spiritual guidance. As an adult, I 
attended a few churches, but real life always seemed to 
get in the way. Recently, I went through something at 
work that seemed to surround me with negativity. 
 
I was stressed, physically burned out, and angry at 
my bosses. My problems at work were manifesting 
themselves in physical ways; to the extent that I was 
experiencing serious medical issues that doctors could 
not solve. It seemed to be affecting every aspect of my 
life. I was becoming consumed by the anger I felt toward 
the people I believed were responsible for everything I 
was going through. 
 
I hated feeling this way and I could not find a way out. 
I decided to turn to prayer. I asked God for guidance. 
I quit my job and almost all of my medical issues went 
away. I was blessed with a new job within a few weeks 
(a miracle considering the pandemic). New, better 
opportunities were presenting themselves. 
 
I felt like I was on the right path, and I had God to 
thank for it. 
 
I turned to Pastor Eric for more guidance. I began 
participating in a bible study. I began watching his 
sermons. I’ve learned how to pray. I’ve learned what it 
means to have a personal relationship with God through 
Jesus Christ. I live my life with confidence, knowing that 
He is there to keep me on the right path, to comfort me 
in times of struggle, and to give me strength when I am 
weak. I know that Jesus died on the cross for my sins 
and has made me a new person. 
  
I know that I can live my life in peace and love 
because I have been saved by God’s grace. 
 
 
TESTIMONY OF QUAN YONGJIE 
 
Dear Brothers and Sisters, 
 
My name is Quan Yongjie and I come from Beijing, 
China (origin: Jilin). I have been in the United States for 
a year now.  I am currently working as a technician in a 
game company. I am happy to share my testimony with 
you today. 
 
I am excited about to be baptized to become a 
Christian. I think back to the journey of my life. I have not 
really experienced any stormy sea. I am just an ordinary 
person who wants to explore the true meaning of life. 
Before believing in Christ, I often thought about what is 
worth pursuing - money, fame, or status? However, I 
have never been able to find the answer. 
 
As a child, I was like thousands of children who grew 
up in China. I always strive to learn and live according to 
the image of a good child in the eyes of my parents, 
elders, and teachers. Although life is not always smooth, 
the result has always been relatively good.  At the time, 
I did not know it was God's care and guidance. Before 
going abroad, I never understood or even thought about 
my goal and direction in life. I went to school, looked for 
a job, got married and started a career step by step, but 
I did not know where my soul was. 
 
On November 3, 2019, I was fortunate enough to 
come Monterey Park Chinese Baptist Church. For the 
first time in my life, I have ever come to church to 
participate in a Christian worship. Before that, I did not 
know anything about Christianity and even had 
preconceived rejection. But when I heard the pastor's 
preaching, his words and behavior have made me feel 
warm and welcomed. I could not help wondering what 
kind of faith can make people so full of joy and love. After 
that, I participated in the Sunday School and later the 
Bible study group. I began to formally study and 
understand everything about faith. I finally realized that 
we are all sinners, and only by trusting in God, that our 
sins can be washed away. Every time in church, the 
peace and harmonious atmosphere, the mutual support 
and love between brothers and sisters, deeply infected 
me. I feel that the church is like a spiritual resting place 
in this chaotic world where the tired soul can take a 
breath. 
 
Just when I was just starting to enjoy this joy, COVID-
19 broke out and it was fierce. It swept the world in just 
a few months. The virus raged ruthlessly and took the 
lives of many people. In order to prevent the spread of 
the pandemic, California began to implement a stay-at-
home order ( It is required to stay at home and not to go 
out) Schools are closed, shops are closed, and social 
activities are prohibited. As a result, the unemployment 
rate has risen sharply. Fortunately, the government 
generously provided a helping hand and began to issue 
relief funds and unemployment benefits, which solved 
the urgent needs of many people. But my situation is 
different. I have just arrived United States for less than 
half a year, I am not eligible for the government's 
assistance. The money I brought from China is running 
out. It is difficult to reach out and ask for money from 
home, the only thing I can do is to go and find a job. 
However, during this period, if you want to work, you had 
to face 2 problems. One is the spread of the virus. There 
is a possibility of getting infected when you go out to 
work. Second, job opportunities are difficult to find. You 
can use the word "rare" to describe it, because basically 
all businesses are closed. Who wants to hire! However, 
even so, I cannot control that much anymore. If I want to 
survive, I had to go out to work. At this time, something 
magical happened. I suddenly cried out "God, my lord, 
help me please! Let me find a job!" Why is it magical? 
Because before I encountered Christianity, people who 
are in difficult times would ask for help from relatives and 
friends, or the heavens and the earth. But not in any 
case, from "God" and "Lord". This is my first time. A voice 
subconsciously cried out from my heart. Later I learned 
that our dialogue with God is through prayer. We pray in

the name of the Lord Jesus Christ. Although I did not 
pray in this manner, it was a prayer from my heart. 
 
After that, I found a job through online recruitment. It 
was not as difficult as I thought. People around me said 
that I was lucky, but I knew that this was the result of 
God's mercy and love for me. In order to thank the Lord 
for his love, I solemnly prayed, thank the Lord for the job 
he has given me, and ask the Lord to continue to love 
me, take care of me, and be with me. Therefore, because 
of the Lord’s kindness and compassion, everything has 
been going on smoothly. Because of the nature of the 
job, I must be in contact with many people every day, but 
the Lord has always been with me, protecting me from 
the virus and keeping safe. Praise the Lord that I have 
finally found a job that is satisfactory! 
 
With the deepening of my studies in church, I 
gradually gained a certain understanding of faith, and 
had a basic knowledge of God’s nature and sin. And 
because of knowing Jesus Christ, I am also quietly 
changing. I have always regarded the Bible as a book 
with a rich philosophy of life, there are still many 
obstacles for me to becoming a believer in it. Because 
we studied natural sciences and we are materialists, it is 
difficult to believe in the invisible and intangible God. In 
my mind, science and Christianity seem to be in 
opposite.  However, advanced science and technology, 
and abundant materials cannot answer the question of 
where we will be. I remember the pastor once said that 
the Bible not only gave birth to modern science, but also 
answered questions beyond science such as the origin 
of life and human development.  Psalm 111:10 says: 
“The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom.” And 
I, a person who thinks I have mastered the tools of 
science, realized how helpless and insignificant I was 
when facing the difficulties in work and life. Well-known 
scientists are witnessing and glorifying God’s wisdom 
with their own personal experience. Why should we then 
not be humble at the feet of God? Jesus said: “‘I have 
come into the world as a light, so that no one who 
believes in me should stay in darkness.” “I am the way 
and the truth and the life.” (John 12:46 and 14:6). In this 
way, the shackles in my heart are gone, the light of God 
illuminates my heart and makes me truly realize the 
sinful nature of myself. Because of the love of the Lord 
Jesus, our sins are forgiven, and by the salvation of 
Jesus Christ, we have eternal life. From then on, my 
mind suddenly became clear. This is the truth I have 
been looking for a long time. Because I have been 
moved inside, deciding to accept Christ as my Savior is 
a natural process. 
 
After the decision, I felt the change in my life. I think 
my wife has the deepest experience of my change. 
Before I became a Christian, I was a very impatient 
person. Because I was separated from my wife, we can 
only communicate via video calls daily, I often got 
impatient with her nagging. Now I pay more attention to 
her emotions, just as said in 1 Corinthians:13, love is 
patient and kind; …. love never ceases. Following the 
words of the Bible, the relationship between my wife and 
I are getting better and better. Although my wife has not 
yet believed in the Lord, and does not have much 
comprehension of our faith, she is happy that I have 
decided to accept Christ as my savior. Praise God! 
 
Thank the Lord Jesus for the redemption on the 
cross. His blood cleansed me from all sins. His 
resurrection gave me new life and made me a child of 
God and a new creation. I would like to follow the Lord 
Jesus all my life and experience God’s love and 
abundance in Christ. I am willing to accept Christ as my 
only savior and Lord of life. I will worship and serve him 
throughout my life. May glory, thanksgiving, and praise 
be to God, the holy trinity! 
 
Finally, I am ending my testimony with this prayer: 
“Heavenly Father, I admit from my heart that you are the 
only true God. You love mankind. You sent your only 
begotten son Jesus Christ to come into the world and be 
crucified for our sins. He was resurrected on the third day 
and saved us from the power of sin and death. I believe 
that Jesus Christ is the only Savior of mankind, the way, 
the truth, and the life. Only through Him can I return to 
you, know the truth, and have eternal life. 
 
I understand that in the past, because I did not know 
you, my direction and course of life were wrong. I 
confess that I am a sinner, and now I have decided to 
turn back and no longer go my own way. I am willing to 
follow Jesus throughout my life and do the Father’s will. 
Please help me understand the truth and strengthen my 
faith. I pray this in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, 
amen!” 
 
 
Quan Yongjie 
Sunday, November 8, 2020

A JOURNEY OF GRACE, A FUTURE OF HOPE 
The forty-ninth anniversary of our church is a day for us to rejoice together before God.
Throughout Scripture, God calls His people to keep feasts and to rejoice in His presence,
because celebration is never mere festivity. It is an act of fellowship in the present: lifting the
same song, giving thanks for the same grace, and realizing that we have not walked this
journey alone. 
As we look back, our hearts are filled with gratitude, for every step of our church’s story bears
the marks of God’s faithful grace. 
As we look ahead, we do not place our confidence in our own strength, but wait in hope for the
greater work God will yet do among us. 
May this celebration deepen our love for one another, renew our thanksgiving for the past, and
strengthen our courage to walk into the future God has prepared. 
Senior Pastor Rev. Caleb Cheung
BY REV. CALEB CHEUNG Ph.D.

together were not just activities or traditions. It was their partnership in the
gospel.

HOW MPCBC AFFECTED MY LIFE
BY DEACON EVELYN HSU
We attended MPCBC but did not join fellowships. The boys attended Diakonos
on occasion if they had a ride.   God was good to us. I did not want to work in
LA because of the commute. But I could not find a job nearby home. When the
boys were born, we decided that I would quit and stay home. But that was not
God’s plan. David’s work was steady but there were times that there was no
work. He would get let go. When the first time he was let go from work; I was
able to go back to the Children’s Hospital to work. During the second time he
was let go; we decided that I should stay working and be responsible for the
health insurance. There was a third time of no work and he was let go. This
lasted for about a year and he was not able to find work. But then he was
called back.
After five years, he was transferred to work in Houston. That was a difficult nine-month period. Houston offered
him a full-time position. The boys were in high school. It would be too difficult to move us all. So, the boys and I
stayed in Los Angeles. During this time, we both learned to trust and have faith in God. We were never without
provisions. We had a home, we had food and we were never without money. We kept two homes afloat,
purchased plane tickets for visiting but we made it a point to constantly tithe for God would provide. 
And God continued to provide. Anson was involved in a serious car accident. He was paralyzed for two weeks.
God performed a miracle and he walked out of the hospital after six weeks. I did not pay any deductible nor co-
pay. Air evacuation was needed but I received no bill for it. I went on a medical mission to Mongolia. While there,
our team was in a severe car accident. The lead doctor required air evacuation for heart surgery in Korea. The
miracle here was that the Korean Surgeon was a classmate of the heart surgeon at the hospital where I worked.
She has fully recovered. The most amazing story was our mission trip to Hong Kong. Our team took months to
prepare the lessons, crafts and games for non-English speaking and non-Christian students. When we arrived,
plans changed. There were no non-English or non-Christian students. They were students attending local
churches. We had twenty-four hours to revise our program. We conquered this task with God’s grace and we
had the best time of our life. God has been good. His plans always work out best. My life has been a blessing
because of MPCBC. All the prayers and support are amazing. Thank you MPCBC.

GROWING THROUGH SERVICE: A JOURNEY HOME
FINDING PURPOSE IN GOD'S HOUSE THROUGH FAITHFUL SERVICE
BY ANSON HSU
The Baptist Faith and Message 2000 reminds us that the church
exists for worship, ministry, and mission. But here's what I've
learned: we don't just attend church—we become the church
through service. "God is working in you to help you want to do
what pleases him.
 Then he gives you the power to do it" (Philippians 2:13 ERV).
Service isn't just about filling roles; it's about filling our hearts
with purpose. When we serve, we discover our spiritual gifts,
build relationships, and experience God's presence.
Prayer: Father, thank You for the gift of service and the privilege
of growing through giving. Help us find our place in Your church
family and use our gifts to glorify You. Amen.
Your church needs your gifts!
Service became my pathway to spiritual growth. Through leading worship and facilitating Monday night Bible
studies at Deacon Evelyn Hsu's home, I discovered that serving others actually serves our own faith journey.
When Eric Kuykendall baptized me, it sealed what service had already begun—a transformed heart fully
committed to Christ and His church.

A note from our pastor 牧者的話

You are welcome here, whichever language you pray in.

We began in 1976 with a handful of families. Today three congregations share this building, and many of our members are the children and grandchildren of the people who started it.

若您初次來訪，請放心前來，隨意入座。我們不會請您站起來自我介紹。門口的同工會為您指引兒童教室與後方停車場。

Say hello 與我們聯絡

We would rather hear from you than guess.

Two cards, both read by a person. A connect card reaches the pastor of the congregation you name, usually within two days. A prayer request goes to the pastors and, if you allow it, to the Wednesday prayer meeting.

Children & families

兒童及家庭事工

Children are welcome in every service. Sunday school runs during the 11:00 hour in English and the 11:30 hour in Mandarin, and a staffed nursery is open all morning for the youngest.

Every volunteer who works with children has completed a background check and our safeguarding training.

Giving 奉獻

Giving here is worship, not a subscription.

Fifty years of offerings built this building, sent our first missionaries, and keep the food pantry open on Saturdays. We do not pass a plate at visitors, and no one is asked for a pledge.

奉獻是感恩的回應，不是責任。

Give online → Set up monthly giving → Mail a check or give by mail →

A 501(c)(3) receipt is mailed every January. Designated gifts — missions, the building fund, benevolence — can be chosen at checkout.

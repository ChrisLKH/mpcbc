Colour proposal

One strong colour for the one action that matters (Plan your visit), one warm colour for kids and highlights, and quiet warm neutrals for everything else. The church logo keeps its own colours — it just stops setting the whole site’s palette. Switch between the two options with the Colour tweak on any design artboard.

Evergreen & Sun

Recommended

Fresh, calm and hopeful — reads as growth and welcome, not “traditional church”. Nothing like the old maroon, so the English-side feedback is answered directly. Neutral enough for grandparents, bright enough for young families.

One colour, one job

The main colour is only for “Plan your visit”, links and the prayer submit. Nothing else competes with it.

No colour per congregation

English, 粵語 and 國語 are told apart by name and photo, not colour — one church, not three brands.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for English headlines, Geist for text, Noto Sans TC / SC (bold) for Chinese — modern, not hymnal.
